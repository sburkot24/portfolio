We recently installed our dyson cordless vacuum charging station.A welcome reprieve as we've struggled with it being semi or out of charge when we've needed it over the past year and a half.

Unfortunately there is not a way to mount the long arm and it liked to fall over which made the whole area appear messy. But luckily Fran Gaur on Makerworld had already solved my problem with their model "[Dyson long tube wall hanger](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2125063-dyson-long-tube-wall-hanger#profileId-2300728)"

Worked like a charm and now only the rest of the house is a mess

Thanks again Fran!
